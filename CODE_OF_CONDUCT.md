# Code of conduct

This is a place to help each other get more out of IssueFlow.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, spam or self-promotion unrelated to the discussion.
- Keep private things private: your own secrets and other people's personal details.
- Stay on topic.

Moderators may edit, hide or lock posts, and remove people who break these rules. To report a problem, email info@xirius.com.
